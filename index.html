💡
What is this? AI agents (like ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, or Siri) are beginning to search, compare prices, and buy items for you. This tracker monitors which websites welcome them and which ones shut them out.

🤖 What are AI Agents?

Unlike normal chatbots that only talk, AI agents can take real actions across the web: adding items to carts, filling checkout forms, or booking reservations on your behalf.

🚫 Why do platforms block them?

Some websites (like Amazon or eBay) want human visitors on their own apps and block bots via robots.txt or terms of service to safeguard revenue, ad views, and data.

🤝 Who is welcoming them?

Partners like Walmart, Etsy, Target and PayPal have launched official Instant Checkout integrations, allowing agents to complete purchases seamlessly with user consent.
